One fortune cookie line for a crypto trader who just got liquidated — written to make them laugh instead of cry.

## The fortune

> You will meet a tall, dark stranger: your liquidation price, already waiting at the door.

15 words, 89 characters, one line. The classic cookie opening ("you will meet a tall, dark stranger") sets up a romance, and the punchline swaps the stranger for the trader's liquidation price — the loss itself, already waiting at the door. Rueful, warm, never cruel.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the fortune to `/output/entry.txt`, where the gate reads it (UTF-8, 1–160 characters, at most 20 words). It validates the word and character caps before writing, so a bad line fails loudly instead of shipping.
